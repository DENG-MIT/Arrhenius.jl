Examples

The repository examples are executable entry points. Some require prepared mechanism sidecars or an optional Julia environment; each script describes its inputs near the top. Run commands from the repository root unless noted.

Reactors

  • Constant-pressure ignition: adiabatic H₂/O₂/Ar ignition with QNDF.
  • Constant-volume ignition: closed constant-volume ignition.
  • Isothermal reactor: compare constant-pressure and constant-volume reactors.
  • Reactor networks: flow devices, stream mixing, heat-transfer walls, and pistons.
  • Engine: a moving-piston model with prescribed injection and valves.
  • Plasma reactors and nanosecond-pulse discharge.

Flames

  • Free premixed flame: transport-stage sequence for H₂/O₂/Ar.
  • Burner-stabilized flame: prescribed inlet mass flux.
  • Fixed-temperature burner flame: measured temperature profile.
  • Counterflow diffusion, opposed premixed, and twin premixed.
  • Inert-wall impinging flame and catalytic impinging flame.
  • Initial guess and restart workflow and ionized flames.

Thermodynamics, transport, and chemistry

  • Thermodynamic calculations: equilibrium, sound speeds, stream mixing, and nozzles.
  • Gas and condensed-phase equilibrium: gas/graphite equilibrium.
  • Real-gas properties: Redlich–Kwong and Helmholtz-based calculations.
  • Parallel transport sweep: viscosity and thermal conductivity over a temperature range.
  • Surface chemistry and catalytic flow: surface kinetics, catalytic reactors, and diamond growth.
  • Electron-energy distribution and Blowers–Masel rates.
  • Mechanism preprocessing: exporters for kinetics, transport, surface, condensed-phase, and real-gas data.
